How Displays Are Run

From briefing to break-off

Every public display and rehearsal follows the same coordination sequence with local ATC.

1

Advance Notice

Display window, altitudes, and track submitted to the controlling vACC ahead of the event.

2

Frequency Assignment

A discrete display/practice frequency is arranged where available to isolate formation traffic.

3

Live Briefing

Formation lead briefs the on-duty controller on composition, manoeuvres, and abort procedures.

4

Display Block

Formation flies the display under ATC's authority, who can amend or hold the sequence at any time.

5

Recovery

Break-off, individual recovery, and hand-back to normal circuit/approach control.

6

Debrief & Logging

Flights logged against pilot VATSIM CIDs for the 10-flights/60-day currency requirement.
