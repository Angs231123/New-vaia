FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a VATSIM account?

Yes — a valid VATSIM CID is required for all rostered pilots and support crew, per our vSOA classification.

Can spectators join a display?

Yes, public display weekends are open to the network. Check the Schedule for dates and frequencies.

Is this affiliated with the real airshow?

No. VAIA is an independent virtual simulation project and is not affiliated with the real-world Australian International Airshow or Avalon Airport.
