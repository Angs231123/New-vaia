What We Do

A fully coordinated virtual airshow

From tight formation passes to solo aerobatic sequences, every VAIA display is planned, rehearsed, and flown under a real operating framework.

✈

Formation Display Flying

Rostered pilots fly choreographed formation passes, tail-chases, and solo aerobatic routines in fast jets and warbirds.

📡

Live ATC Coordination

Every display window is pre-briefed with the controlling vACC/ARTCC, with dedicated display frequencies and NOTAM-style bulletins.

🛬

Home At Avalon (YMAV)

Based at Avalon Airport, Victoria — home of the real Australian International Airshow — with hangar ops and static displays.

📋

Currency & Standards

Rostered pilots maintain currency with 10 flights every 60 days under the team callsign to stay display-qualified.

🎓

Pilot Development

Structured work-ups from solo circuits to two-ship and four-ship formation before display clearance.

🌐

VATSIM Network

Operating openly on the VATSIM network under the vSOA framework for special events and demonstration teams.

The Hangar

Built by a crew that flies together

VAIA started as a handful of virtual pilots who wanted to bring the atmosphere of Avalon's international airshow into the sim world — real coordination, real currency standards, and a genuine sense of occasion for every display.
